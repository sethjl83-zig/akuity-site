Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Akuity is a language learning app developed by Zigollzo LLC. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use Akuity.

Data We Collect

Akuity does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — including your name, vocabulary lists, and study progress — is stored locally on your device only and never sent to any server.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using standard mobile storage. This data is never transmitted to Zigollzo LLC or any third party. If you delete the app, all stored data is permanently removed from your device.

Analytics and Tracking

Akuity does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not track your usage, location, or behavior.

Children's Privacy

Akuity does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the updated version here with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@akuity.app.
